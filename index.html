Let's talk about AI, because I know you're wondering.

Yes, I use it. I'd rather tell you up front than have you wonder.

I know a lot of folks around here are leery of it, and I get it. Nobody wants their business sounding like a robot wrote it. I don't want that either.

Here's what it really does. AI is a tool, and I lean on it a lot. It does much of the research and first drafting, and it helps me look at your numbers to see what's working so I can adjust. Honestly, it gets my own wheels turning. It helps my creativity. It doesn't replace it. And it doesn't know you or your customers. That part comes from you, and from me.
